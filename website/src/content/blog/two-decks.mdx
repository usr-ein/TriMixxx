One deck is a prototype. Two decks are a booth, or at least half of one. September went into a second chassis, and on 1 October a second deck was built in it.

## The second chassis

The first chassis had copied a CDJ-3000's whole envelope. The second, drawn in September, is lower and tighter: **423 × 329 × 85 mm**, with the screen tilted back behind the jog wheel and the pads, and the PLAY and CUE buttons down to 33 mm.

The screen is a Waveshare 10.1-inch DSI panel, 1280 × 800, with touch. Its mounting was measured from the panel itself once it had arrived, and the notes are glad of that order of events: designing it from the datasheet first would have gone wrong. On the back panel are an IEC mains inlet feeding a 5 V module inside, an RJ45 coupler for the Pro DJ Link cable, and the sound card's RCA sockets.

## Bringing up trimixxx2

The second deck was assembled on 1 October, and its bring-up was written down step by step as it happened, from a freshly flashed card to a working deck: what a new unit needs, in order, so the third won't need anyone's memory.

The best moment of the day was a button that did nothing. BACK seemed dead. Pressing every control on the deck in order, with a MIDI monitor running, showed why: this deck's ring A had an **eighth pad**, third in its chain. Every pad after it arrived one note late, so ÷2 was opening the library and BACK was sending a note nobody listened to. Its hot cues arrived in reverse order too, and its jog wheel turned the other way.

## An accent colour each

Two decks on a table look identical from a distance, so each got its own **accent colour**: the selection in the browser, the header's rule, the key, the BPM, the remaining time. trimixxx1 keeps the skin's lime. trimixxx2 is pink. The emulated deck, which is a virtual copy of trimixxx2, is cyan, so a screenshot never says the wrong deck.

<Photo id="2026-10-09-img-9295" />

The same file also says whether the panel sits behind a lip or flush with the case, so the skin knows whether to keep its edges clear. And the pads were dimmed to a perceived 80 % of full, which, because eyes aren't linear, is about 61 % of full drive.

## A splash in eight seconds

On power-on, trimixxx2's screen stayed black for about twenty seconds before the logo. A day of measuring found where they went:

- the bootloader showed a network-install screen on every cold boot, which cost more than five seconds, until the Pi's EEPROM was told not to;
- the panel waited for its drivers to load from the root filesystem, until they were put in the initramfs, which brought the panel up 1.66 s after the kernel started instead of 6.15;
- the swap configuration ran a Perl script on every boot, until its sizes were pinned.

Counted by hand from the power switch, the logo now appears after about **8 seconds**, and the whole boot takes 15.3 seconds, down from 22.9, and from 49.5 on a fresh card. The splash still lets the boot log print behind it: on a deck about to play a gig, a service that failed to start should be visible before the music needs it.

<Callout kind="note" title="Changing the boot path without locking yourself out">
  Every one of these boot changes was tried first through the Pi's own one-shot <code>tryboot</code>
  : a candidate configuration that runs once, and if it fails, the next power cycle comes back as
  before. A deck you can't plug a keyboard into has to be changed this way.
</Callout>

## A network of its own

In a club there is no known Wi-Fi to join, and a deck nobody can reach can't be fixed. So if the deck has joined no network 45 seconds into its boot, it becomes a **hotspot** named after itself, on its own 2.4 GHz channel, once per boot, with nothing waiting on it. On trimixxx2, a phone joined it and logged into the deck over ssh.

The next two days added the rest of what a deck needs when something goes wrong: the touchscreen rescue console, every light switched off at shutdown, because the controller stays powered after the Pi halts and the last lit lamp used to stay lit, and a Diagnostics page that can set the deck's output level and its screen's brightness.

Three days later, trimixxx2 went to a gig.
