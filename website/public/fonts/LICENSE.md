# Fonts

`meslo-lgl-*.woff2` are Meslo LG L, the deck's own interface font, subset to
the characters the site uses. Meslo LG is André Berg's customisation of Apple's
Menlo, itself based on Bitstream Vera Sans Mono, and is published under the
Apache License 2.0 (<https://github.com/andreberg/Meslo-Font>). These come from
the Nerd Fonts build the deck ships (`mixxx_config/fonts`), whose patches are
under the MIT licence (<https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts>).
