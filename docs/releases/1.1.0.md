# TriMixxx 1.1.0

The deck's release after 1.0.2 (2026-10-17). **The S3 moves to another
UART, so each unit must be rewired before it gets 1.1.0:** its two wires go
from header pins 8 and 10 to pins 7 and 29. That gives the Pi's own
Bluetooth its UART back, and the deck becomes a Bluetooth speaker for a phone,
from "Bluetooth receiver" in the home list. It also fixes a boot that could
end on a black screen.

A minor release: the Y of Sam's X.Y.Z (`pi-qemu/README.md`, "Cards and
releases"). The rewiring is why: "It's bumping the minor since we switched the
UART of the S3, quite a major change" (Sam).

## Before updating: rewire the S3

Once per unit, at the Pi's 40-pin header, with the deck off:

| The wire | From | To |
|---|---|---|
| Pi TX → S3 RX (IO44) | pin 8 (GPIO 14, TXD0) | **pin 7 (GPIO 4, TXD3)** |
| Pi RX ← S3 TX (IO43) | pin 10 (GPIO 15, RXD0) | **pin 29 (GPIO 5, RXD3)** |

GND and 5 V stay where they are. Nothing changes on the S3 board or in its
firmware.

- **A unit not rewired loses its controls on 1.1.0**, and a rewired unit
  loses them on 1.0.x. The move is a plain one, made once, with no way back
  (Sam). The other slot's 1.0.x, reached by Diagnostics' RESTART INTO or a
  rollback, then has no S3.
- trimixxx2 was rewired on 2026-10-17. trimixxx1 isn't yet: it must not get
  1.1.0 before it is. trimixxx3 has no S3.
- After the update, `pinctrl get 4,5,14,15` shows 4/5 as `TXD3`/`RXD3` and
  14/15 as inputs.

## For the DJ

- **Bluetooth receiver**, in the home list with the Bluetooth rune. While its
  page is open, the deck is a Bluetooth speaker. Mixxx lets go of the sound
  card, the phone plays straight to the DAC, and Mixxx takes the card back
  when you leave (BACK).
  - **The deck stays still:** the page holds it paused, and the home list
    row refuses to open while the deck plays ("Stop the deck first"), since
    a wrong row mid-set would be silence. In the page, PLAY, CUE, the loops,
    the pads and the jog do nothing. The encoder and BACK move around, and
    the tempo fader is still followed.
  - **Pairing:** press Pair (the deck is visible to phones for two minutes),
    choose the deck on the phone (its name is the deck's hostname). Then
    confirm the same six digits on both.
  - **Picking:** with nothing playing, the page lists the paired phones.
    **Nothing connects until you pick one**, every time the page opens, and
    only the phone picked plays. Another paired phone trying to connect or
    take over is refused until you pick it. Picking another switches at
    once. A phone just paired is the one picked. While a phone plays,
    Devices brings the list back.
  - **Forgetting:** Forget, or a long press on a row, asks "Forget …?", then
    unpairs the phone. It has to pair again to play here.
  - **What plays:** the phone's cover (or the Bluetooth rune when it sends
    none), title, artist, album, and a position that moves. That comes from
    the phone over AVRCP. A Mac sends no track info at all (it was tried), so
    the page shows its name and "Playing from …".
  - **Screen off:** the Screen off button, or three minutes without a touch,
    blanks the panel and every S3 light. Any touch or control wakes them,
    and does nothing else.
  - **The radio is on only while the page is open.**
- **No more black screen at boot.** A deck could start with a black screen
  and stay that way, Mixxx never starting, though ssh worked. It happened
  when the deck's session started during the boot splash's last seconds,
  which a quick boot does (trimixxx2, moved and on Wi-Fi only, did it on
  every boot). Any deck could hit it, 1.0.2 included. On 1.0.x, a session
  restart brings Mixxx back:
  `ssh DECK 'sudo systemctl reset-failed getty@tty1; sudo chvt 1; sudo systemctl restart getty@tty1'`.

## The deck's system

- **The S3 on UART3:** `config.txt` has `dtoverlay=uart3` in place of
  `enable_uart=1` and `dtoverlay=disable-bt`, and ttymidi opens
  `/dev/ttyAMA3`. The base step deletes the two old lines from a card that
  has them.
- **Bluetooth on UART0**, as on a stock Pi: the kernel attaches the radio by
  itself (krnbt, `hci_uart_bcm`), and it stays soft-blocked (rfkill) until
  the page opens.
- **New packages:** `bluez-alsa-utils` (BlueALSA, A2DP sink, and
  `bluealsa-aplay` to the DAC) and `bluez-obexd` (covers, over AVRCP's
  imaging). Their own units are masked; the deck's units in
  `pi_config/bluetooth/` start only when Mixxx runs
  `sudo -n trimixxx-bluetooth up` from the page, and a guard takes it all
  down if that Mixxx dies. Nothing Bluetooth starts at boot.
- **Paired phones are kept on p8:** `/var/lib/rauc/trimixxx-bluetooth` is
  bind-mounted over `/var/lib/bluetooth`. It is written when a phone pairs,
  is picked (BlueZ keeps the trust) or is forgotten; a normal start writes
  nothing (invariant 15).
- **Covers through the stock mpris-proxy's session:** Pi OS's mpris-proxy
  opens a cover session to any phone that offers covers, and Android's cover
  server takes one client at all, so Mixxx takes the cover through that
  session rather than opening a second one that would be refused.
- **The session brings tty1 forward before X** (`pi_config/bash_profile`),
  which is the black screen's fix.
- **RAUC's tryboot** mounts the boot selector read-write only to write
  `autoboot.txt`, and `/data` is never checked at start (from main since
  1.0.2).
- **Mixxx is 1.0.2's** but for the fork's commits since, all the receiver's:
  - SoundManager can let go of every device until `setupDevices()`
  - the receiver itself, its page and the home list's row
  - the library hidden with the page up takes its level with it
  - the cover through a session already open to the phone
  - picking and forgetting, and the row refused while the deck plays

## The tooling

- **pi-qemu emulates the BCM2711's UART2–5** (QEMU patch 22) and puts the
  emulated S3 on UART3 when the card enables it. **The main checkout's QEMU
  must be rebuilt** (`pi-qemu/build.sh qemu`) before an emulated deck can run
  a 1.1.0 card. Until then, the card is refused with "this card puts the S3
  on UART3, which this QEMU lacks".
- A BlueZ stand-in and an obexd stand-in (`pi_config/bluetooth/`) play phones
  that pair, connect, play, offer covers and are refused, so the page can be
  tried on an emulated deck with no radio.
- From main since 1.0.2:
  - `deck ship` shows its progress
  - each card says what code it was built from
  - emulated decks start from main's latest
  - the golden is remade after a merge, and a stuck remake holds no build
  - the crew's own changes
  - trimixxx.e1n.sh, the website

## Updating

- **Rewire the unit first** (above).
- From 1.0.2, the update is RAUC's bundle, as before:
  `pi-qemu deck ship --host ALIAS-eth --version 1.1.0`, over the deck's cable
  (over its Wi-Fi only when the person says so: `--host ALIAS --wifi`). The
  deck installs it into its other slot and starts it as a trial. Its health
  check keeps it once the network and ssh are up, or the deck goes back.
- From 1.0.1 or earlier, everything in those releases' notes comes with it.
- The S3's firmware is unchanged: nothing to flash.
- A new card is a deck's own, `pi-qemu release card DECK --version 1.1.0`,
  flashed from its `.img.xz`. The release's card, `trimixxx-1.1.0.img.xz`,
  holds no deck's identity.

## Tried

- **On trimixxx2, rewired, real**, with rehearsal releases of this work
  (1.0.2-bt1, then 1.0.2-bt2):
  - The S3 on UART3: Sam's presses arrived and Mixxx's lights went back.
  - Bluetooth with Sam's Mac: pairing with the code confirmed on the deck,
    the stream to the DAC, screen off and waking, BACK and Mixxx playing
    again, the Mac back with no code when the page reopened. The Mac sends
    no track info (a capture of its AVRCP shows it).
  - The black screen: on 1.0.2-bt1, every one of Sam's reboots ended black.
    On 1.0.2-bt2, six boots in a row (the trial and five more) came up with
    Mixxx on the screen, every one of them starting its session inside the
    splash's last seconds.
- **On emulated decks**, with the stand-ins: the page, pairing, picking two
  phones and switching between them, a phone refused when it connects
  without being picked, forgetting, nothing connecting on reopening, covers
  through mpris-proxy's session, screen off and waking, the audio switch,
  and the row refused while the deck plays.
- **Not yet:** a phone's track info and cover on a real deck (the Mac sends
  neither), and trimixxx1, which isn't rewired.
