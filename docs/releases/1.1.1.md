# TriMixxx 1.1.1

The deck's release after 1.1.0 (2026-10-18). It brings the Bluetooth
receiver's fixes from Sam playing his iPhone through it:
- the device's cover shows;
- PLAY and the loop pads drive the device, and the page has its own
  previous, play/pause and next;
- every paired device has a star and a bin, and the starred one connects by
  itself when the page opens;
- the page says "device", never "phone", since a Mac plays too.

A patch release: the Z of Sam's X.Y.Z (`pi-qemu/README.md`, "Cards and
releases"). There's nothing to rewire.

## For the DJ

- **Covers.** An iPhone sends a track's cover only while the deck holds a
  cover session open to it. 1.1.0 never opened one, so no cover came. The deck
  now opens one as soon as the device is in, and each new track brings its
  cover.
  - The track that is already playing when the device connects may show the
    rune until the next track.
  - A device that offers no covers still shows the rune.
- **The deck's controls drive the device** while the page is open:
  - **PLAY** plays or pauses it, from what the device says it is doing. Its
    light shows whether the device plays.
  - **Loop halve** goes to its previous track, **loop double** to its next.
  - Every other control still does nothing in the page. With the screen off,
    any control only wakes it.
- **The same on the page:** previous, play/pause and next under the time bar,
  by touch or with the encoder. Play/pause shows ❚❚ while the device plays,
  ▶ while it doesn't.
- **The time follows the device:** a track change, a pause and a play show at
  once. A seek on the device itself (dragging Spotify's time back to 0:00)
  shows at the device's next pause, play or track change, and the deck's own
  PLAY and loop pads count. Between those, the deck counts on from the last
  true time.
  - An iPhone tells nothing of a seek (Apple's accessory guidelines list the
    events iOS sends, and a position change isn't one of them), and the
    deck's BlueZ asks only after such an event.
  - Following a seek at once would take a patched BlueZ, which wasn't done.
- **The device list:**
  - **+** in its corner pairs a new device. It is 1.1.0's Pair, and turns
    blue-ringed while the deck is visible.
  - Each paired device has **a star** and **a bin**. The bin asks "Forget …?"
    first, as a long press on the row does. 1.1.0's Forget button and its
    "which?" step are gone.
  - The encoder goes through everything on the page in reading order: a
    row, its star, its bin, then the next row.
- **The starred device** (one at most; starring another moves the star,
  tapping it again takes it off):
  - When the page opens, the deck connects it by itself. While it isn't in
    (out of range, its Bluetooth off), the deck tries again every 10 s,
    saying "Connecting to …".
  - Picking another device stands until the page closes.
  - Never outside the page: the radio is off then.
- **"Device", never "phone":** "No device paired yet", "Pick a device",
  "Playing from …", and so on.

## The deck's system

- **bluetoothd runs experimental** (`bluetooth-main.conf`).
  - BlueZ 5.82 shows a device's cover port (`MediaPlayer1.ObexPort`) only
    then, and without it no cover session is opened.
  - The mode also turns on BlueZ's other experimental parts: the LE Audio
    profiles (BAP, BASS, VCP, MICP, MCP, CSIP, ASHA; BAP's streams also
    need the kernel's ISO sockets, which stay off), the admin-policy driver
    and the advertisement-monitor manager. They act only for an LE Audio
    device or a client that asks for them. A phone or a Mac plays over A2DP
    and AVRCP, and pairs, as before.
  - The admin-policy driver writes its settings once, the first time the
    page opens: an empty service allowlist (which allows every service),
    `admin_policy_settings`, beside the pairings on p8.
- **The star is kept beside the pairings,** on p8 on a release card
  (`/var/lib/rauc/trimixxx-bluetooth/trimixxx-starred`, one address).
  - The helper writes it whole or not at all: `trimixxx-bluetooth star
    ADDRESS|none`, which its sudoers now allows. `up` reads it back.
  - It is written only when the DJ stars, unstars or forgets the starred
    device.
  - The starred device stays trusted between openings, so BlueZ writes
    nothing for it each time the page opens.
- **Mixxx is 1.1.0's** plus one commit of the fork's: the cover session, the
  device's controls, the position, the star, the list's + / star / bin, and
  the wording.

## The tooling

- **The stand-ins play an iPhone:**
  - covers only from the next track, once a cover session is open;
  - the player's Play, Pause, Next and Previous, answered and logged;
  - and for the deck's tests, Seek, Status, and Away / Near (out of range:
    a Connect fails after BlueZ's 5 s page timeout).

## Updating

- **From 1.1.0:**
  - `pi-qemu deck ship --host ALIAS-eth --version 1.1.1`, over the deck's
    cable (over its Wi-Fi only when the person says so: `--host ALIAS
    --wifi`).
  - The deck installs it into its other slot and starts it as a trial, and
    its health check keeps it once the network and ssh are up.
- **From 1.0.x:** 1.1.0's notes first. The unit must be rewired (pins 7 and
  29) before it gets 1.1.x. trimixxx1 isn't rewired yet, so it must not get
  1.1.1.
- The S3's firmware is unchanged: there's nothing to flash.
- A new card is a deck's own, `pi-qemu release card DECK --version 1.1.1`.

## Tried

- **On trimixxx2, with Sam's iPhone, on 1.1.0:**
  - Its AVRCP record offers covers (AVRCP 1.6, the cover-art bit, BIP on
    PSM 0x1009), but BlueZ hid the port.
  - With a cover session opened by hand, each track change brought its cover
    (a 200 px JPEG, in about a second).
  - A seek sent nothing; the pause after it brought the true position.
- **On emulated decks**, with the stand-ins:
  - the cover from the next track once a session is open;
  - PLAY pausing and playing the device, its light following;
  - loop double and halve, and the page's previous / play-pause / next,
    skipping and pausing;
  - a seek back to 0:00 followed at once;
  - the list's +, star and bin;
  - the starred device connected on reopening, tried every 10 s while away,
    and in once back.
