# TriMixxx 1.0.2

The deck's release after 1.0.1, made the same day (2026-10-15). Its own system
no longer shows up on the other decks as a stick called "root-ro", with the
ALSA test sounds as its music. And the deck serves its sticks only to the
players on its cable: never on its Wi-Fi, and with no cable in it is not a
player at all.

A bug-fix release: the Z of Sam's X.Y.Z (`pi-qemu/README.md`, "Cards and
releases").

## For the DJ

- **No more "root-ro".** A deck on 1.0.0 or 1.0.1 offered its own system to
  every player on the link as a USB stick called `root-ro`: the other deck
  listed it as a source ("root-ro inserted — browsing folders", 9 tracks in
  `usr/share/sounds/alsa`), and a CDJ would have too. On 1.0.2 a deck
  offers only the sticks plugged into it. **Until both decks run 1.0.2,** a
  1.0.2 deck still lists a 1.0.1 deck's `root-ro`: the fix is in what a deck
  offers, not in what it lists.
- **A stick plugged in takes the USB slot again.** `root-ro` held it from
  the deck's start, so a real stick plugged in later was offered in the SD
  slot.
- **With no cable in, a deck is not a player on the Wi-Fi any more.** It
  was one on whatever network it had: at home on the home Wi-Fi, at a venue
  on its own hotspot, offering its sticks to whoever was there. It now waits
  for its Ethernet cable and is a player within seconds of it: about 1.5 s
  to take the link, and the usual 10 s to claim its number. Two decks see
  each other over a cable only, as CDJs do.
- **Diagnostics says where the deck is a player:** a Pro DJ Link row under
  Network, "player 3 on eth0", or "waiting for eth0" with no cable in.

## The deck

- **A plain stick is a USB disk** (prolink fa081f9). The Pro DJ Link side
  offered every mount point under `/media` as a stick of plain files, and a
  release card mounts its read-only root's two layers there: overlayroot's
  `/media/root-ro` (the squashfs on the card) and `/media/root-rw` (the RAM
  over it). It now offers only what the kernel's mount table says is a USB
  disk (`/dev/sd*`), the rule the deck's own browser has kept since the
  first release. Offering the root meant walking it, 74,478 files, which
  took trimixxx2 7 s once it had joined the link, and it put every file the
  deck's user can read in the NFS export: readable by any player on the
  link and, with no cable in, by anyone on the deck's Wi-Fi.
- **The dbserver answers only on the cable** (prolink 3e1c92f). Its two
  ports, 1051 and the port query on 12523, listened on every interface, the
  Wi-Fi included, while the NFS server's were already held to the link.
  Both are now pinned to the interface the deck is a player on.
- **The deck is a player on eth0 only** (prolink ae895ad,
  usr-ein/mixxx@9fa9117): `[ProLink] Interface eth0` in `mixxx.cfg`. Mixxx
  used to take the best interface it could find, the link-local one first
  and then any private address, so with no cable in it moved to the Wi-Fi
  within 2 s.
- **Mixxx is 1.0.1's** but for these: the fork's two commits since,
  usr-ein/mixxx@0101602 (prolink's three) and usr-ein/mixxx@9fa9117 (the
  interface and the Diagnostics row).

## Updating

- From 1.0.1, the update is RAUC's bundle, as before:
  `pi-qemu deck ship --host ALIAS-eth --version 1.0.2`, over the deck's
  cable. The deck installs it into its other slot and starts it as a trial.
  Its health check keeps it once the network and ssh are up, or the deck
  goes back to 1.0.1.
- **Update both decks.** A deck still on 1.0.1 or 1.0.0 goes on offering
  `root-ro`, and a 1.0.2 deck beside it goes on listing it.
- From 1.0.0 or 0.1.1, everything in those releases' notes comes with it.
- The S3's firmware hasn't changed since 0.1.1: nothing to flash.
- The way back is Diagnostics' RESTART INTO, to whichever release the other
  slot holds.
- A new card is a deck's own, `pi-qemu release card DECK --version 1.0.2`,
  flashed from its `.img.xz`. The release's card, `trimixxx-1.0.2.img.xz`,
  holds no deck's identity.

## Tried on emulated decks only

Found on trimixxx2 (1.0.1), read over its Wi-Fi: its log, its mounts and its
database showed trimixxx1's `root-ro` listed as player 4's stick, and
trimixxx2 serving its own. Then shown on emulated decks started from 1.0.1's
release card, the only kind with overlayroot's mounts:

- on 1.0.1, each of two linked decks listed the other's `root-ro`, a plain
  stick took the SD slot beside it, the dbserver answered from a second
  interface, and a pulled cable moved the deck onto that interface;
- on 1.0.2's Mixxx and `mixxx.cfg`, no `root-ro` either way, the stick in
  the USB slot and still a Folders source, Record set's picker listing only
  the deck's stick, nothing answering from the second interface while
  everything did on the link, and with the cable pulled no player and
  nothing listening, Diagnostics "waiting for eth0", back as player 4 11.5 s
  after the cable;
- two emulated CDJ-2000NXSs on the link browsed the deck's plain stick over
  LINK, loaded a track from it and played it.

No real deck or real CDJ has run it yet.
