# TriMixxx 1.0.0

The deck's release after 0.1.1 (2026-10-09). For the DJ it brings a History
of what played on the link, Record set, KEY SYNC with a list of keys, plain
sticks shared with CDJs both ways, and a Pro DJ Link that behaves as two CDJs
do with each other. Underneath, the fork's C++ is refactored to its map, and
pi-qemu runs Pioneer's own CDJ-2000NXS firmware beside the deck.

From 1.0.0 on, versions follow Sam's scheme: X for a large change, such as a
new feature; Y for smaller features and refactors; Z for bug fixes; and he
decides which (`pi-qemu/README.md`, "Cards and releases").

## For the DJ

- **History, on the home list** (#37). What played on the Pro DJ Link
  network during this power-up, the one before and the one before that,
  whoever played it: this deck, another TriMixxx, a CDJ. Each power-up reads
  as a timeline, the latest row at the top: the tracks that played together
  and for how long (`Track 1 | Track 2  20s`), each with its cover, its artist
  and the stick it came from. The card keeps the last three power-ups.
- **Record set, on the home list** (#38). It records the DAC's line input
  (the UCA222's RCA in) as an MP3 onto the card, never into RAM. While it
  records, the row reads **End recording**, with the time so far and a meter
  of L and R. Ending it writes the set to one of the deck's own sticks,
  `recorded-set-NNNNNNNN.mp3` with the History's track list for that time
  beside it, or discards it. Three hours at most, and after five silent
  minutes the deck asks whether to end it. The card's copy is gone at the
  next boot.
- **KEY SYNC on B6** (#36). A press over the deck puts the track on the
  master's Camelot number: one shift of -5 to +6 semitones, in the track's
  own mode. Another press, or a press with nothing to match, puts it back in
  its own key. A hold opens the twelve keys the track can be pitched to,
  master or not, and the encoder or a tap picks one. The pad stays lit while
  the deck is out of the track's own key.
- **Plain sticks, both ways with CDJs** (#39). A stick of music files with no
  rekordbox library, in the deck, is shared on the link as a CDJ shares one:
  a CDJ browses it by FOLDER, sees each file's tags and cover, loads it and
  plays it. A CDJ's plain stick is a Folders source on the deck, and the
  History names what a CDJ plays off one. A CDJ shows the deck's stick at its
  real size.
- **Pro DJ Link as two CDJs do it** (#33), from the CDJ-to-CDJ captures. A
  CDJ beside the deck no longer re-claims its player number every few
  seconds. A tempo-master hand-over runs at a CDJ's pace. A stopped master
  hands master on, and the first deck to play takes an empty mastership. The
  deck asks a CDJ about its sticks as a CDJ does, rather than every 5 s.
- **Diagnostics shows the release** (#20): its version, commit and date, the
  A/B slot it started from (as a trial or not, kept or not), what the other
  slot holds, and the uptime. **RESTART INTO** the other slot restarts the
  deck into it as a trial, after a confirm, and the health check keeps it or
  rolls it back as it does an update.
- **A quick second load just loads** (#23). A load within half a second of
  the last was taken for a double tap, and lost; Autoplay no longer waits
  0.6 s to dodge it. A load that replaces one still opening off a slow stick
  now loads too, where it left the deck empty.

## The deck's system

- The History and Record set keep their files on p8, the state partition both
  slots share and updates leave alone: `/var/lib/rauc/trimixxx-history`, made
  at every start by `trimixxx-history.service`, and
  `/var/lib/rauc/trimixxx-recording`, emptied at every start by
  `trimixxx-recording.service`. A release's root leaves `/var/lib/rauc`'s
  content out.
- `dj-usb write` is the one command that writes to a stick, Record set's, run
  by Mixxx through one sudoers line for exactly it. The stick is read-write
  for the copy only, written 16 MB at a time, and the other port waits for
  it.
- trimixxx-launchd takes `F0 7D 03 "SLOT" F7`: it marks the other slot active
  and restarts the deck into it (Diagnostics' RESTART INTO).
- `/etc/trimixxx-release` gives the commit's hash (`HASH=`) and when the
  release was sealed (`BUILT=`).
- The fork's own C++ is refactored to its map, `docs/fork-map.md` (#24): one
  owner for the deck's services, three classes split along their jobs, the
  dead Pro DJ Link sidebar gone; 1.75k lines shorter, and 45 upstream files
  touched instead of 55. Nothing changes for the DJ.
- prolink 0.5.0, from 0.3.0 and 27 commits; Mixxx 2.5.6 underneath, as
  before.

## Updating from 0.1.1

- The update is RAUC's bundle, as before: `pi-qemu deck ship --host ALIAS
  --version 1.0.0`. The deck installs it into its other slot and starts it as
  a trial. Its health check keeps it once the network and ssh are up, or the
  deck goes back to 0.1.1.
- The S3's firmware hasn't changed since 0.1.1: nothing to flash.
- The way back is Diagnostics' RESTART INTO, on 1.0.0. 0.1.1 has no such
  button: from it, `sudo rauc status mark-active other && sudo systemctl
  reboot` over ssh does the same.
- A new card is a deck's own, `pi-qemu release card DECK --version 1.0.0`,
  flashed from its `.img.xz`. The release's card, `trimixxx-1.0.0.img.xz`,
  holds no deck's identity: it starts as `trimixxx`, with no Wi-Fi and no ssh.

## Tried on emulated decks only

All of the above was tried on emulated decks, and the Pro DJ Link changes on
emulated CDJ-2000NXS running Pioneer's own firmware. A real deck has run
Diagnostics with its RESTART INTO (trimixxx1, #20) and the refactored fork's
start-up (trimixxx1, #24). Still to be shown on real hardware:

- Record set from the real UCA222's input, with its underruns, throttling and
  write times (#38's hardware check);
- the Pro DJ Link changes against real CDJs (`docs/prolink-learnings.md` §5,
  and `docs/plain-usb-plan.md` §7 for plain sticks);
- the History and KEY SYNC on a real deck.

## Tooling, on the Mac

- **Emulated CDJs**, `pi-qemu cdj`: a CDJ-2000NXS on Pioneer's own firmware,
  on the decks' links (#26), at a real one's speed, playing a track at its
  tempo with `--dsp-model` (#28), in a window with the lamps its firmware
  lights (#29), with a quickstart, `docs/cdj-emulator-quickstart.md` (#35).
  The emulator's real-time fixes are offered upstream (#34). Captures of two
  emulated CDJs sit beside prolink's hardware corpus (#31, #32).
- **pi-qemu:** `deck alias` writes a real deck's Ethernet as `ALIAS-eth`
  (#25); `deck ship` refuses a dev card; an emulated deck has the DAC's line
  input and a stick it may write to; a release's cards are `.img.xz`, image
  builds are trimmed, and a deck's own trim gives its space back to the Mac
  (#40); `.cache/` and `archives/` at the repo's root, and `pi-qemu cache` for
  every cache (#41).
- **Mixxx's Docker build** is lighter on the Mac: a cold build peaks at 7
  compilers instead of 25, and a one-file deploy takes ~22 s instead of ~35
  (#40).
- **The crew**, the agents that take Sam's pitches to merged PRs: the
  nightman adopts a session Sam started (#27), emulated CDJs have limits
  (#30), real decks go to one agent at a time under a lock per unit, by night
  too, and a pitch can have its plan reviewed first.
